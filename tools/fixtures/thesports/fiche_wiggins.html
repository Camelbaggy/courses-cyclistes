Cycling - Bradley Wiggins

Great Britain - 28 April 1980

Bradley Wiggins (born 28 April 1980 in Ghent) is a professional road racing cyclist who competes internationally for Great Britain. Bradley Wiggins is professional from 2001. His last victories are the stage 3b in the Driedaagse De Panne-Koksijde 2015 and the men's time trial in the World Championships 2014.
